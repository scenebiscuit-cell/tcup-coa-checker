How THC is counted

The statute caps "tetrahydrocannabinols," not just Δ9-THC. Total THC here is Δ9-THC + Δ8-THC + Δ10-THC + THCA × 0.877. Labs often print a Total THC that leaves out Δ8, so the checker sums the individual rows itself.

Rule text vs. statute

Published copies of 37 TAC §12.7(q) still read 0.5% THC and 10% CBD by weight. The legislature has since replaced that standard, most recently with HB 46's 10 mg per dosage unit and 1 g per package. Statute controls, so the checker applies the HB 46 limits.

Contaminant limits

TCUP rules list what must be tested but defer to Texas Department of Agriculture standards rather than publishing their own action-limit table. Each result is compared to the limit printed on the COA.

Run with a local model

1. Install Ollama and run ollama pull qwen2.5:14b. For scanned COAs and photos, also pull a vision model such as qwen2.5vl:7b.

2. Let this site talk to Ollama. On Windows, run setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS "https://your-site" in PowerShell, then quit Ollama from the system tray and start it again.

3. Open Use a local AI model above and click Connect. If your browser asks to let the site reach apps on this device, allow it.
